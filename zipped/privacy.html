Zipped privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Zipped keeps your lists on your own phone and watch. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and the app cannot use the internet.

Zipped is a packing checklist app for Android phones and Wear OS watches, published by SBA Developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app stores

The lists you make, the items in them, and your log of finished packs: which items were checked, when, and on which device. The app also remembers your theme setting and when your phone and watch last synced.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to me or to anyone else. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service of its own, and it does not have permission to use the internet.

Your data moves in only two ways, both under your control:

  • Between your phone and your watch. If you have Zipped on a Wear OS watch paired with your phone, your lists and log are synced between the two through Google's Wear OS Data Layer, part of Google Play services on your devices.
  • Android backup. If you have backup turned on in Android's settings, your lists, log and theme setting are included in your device's backup to your own Google account, and in a transfer to a new device. You can turn this off in Android's settings.

Google handles those two under its own privacy policy.

Crash and performance reports

If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google in Android's settings, Google Play gives me anonymous crash and performance statistics for the app. They do not include your lists.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting a list or a log entry in the app removes it from your phone and watch. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes everything it has stored on that device.
